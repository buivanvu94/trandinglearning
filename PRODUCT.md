# PRODUCT.md — Trading Masterclass Learning Platform

## Product Overview
- **Name:** Trading Masterclass (Price Action, Volume Profile & Cung Cầu)
- **Category:** Educational Pro Web Application / Chart Analysis Study Suite
- **Primary Goal:** Provide an impeccable, museum-grade learning platform for mastering 14 in-depth trading modules and 164 high-resolution 2K charts.

## Audience & Persona
- **Audience:** Serious financial traders, technical analysts, and students of Price Action, Orderflow, and Volume Profile.
- **Tone & Voice:** Apple Pro — quiet, authoritative, ultra-refined, distraction-free. The UI chrome recedes completely, letting the detailed 2K chart artifacts take center stage.
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Dark gallery canvas (#000000 / #161617 / #1d1d1f), SF Pro typography, signature Apple Action Blue (#2997ff), subtle translucent pill capsules, zero visual clutter or loud neon gradients.

## Core Surfaces
1. **Curriculum Dashboard (`/`):**
   - High-level progress tracking and quick resume action.
   - 14 curriculum cards with crisp preview tiles, progress bars, and filters.
2. **Interactive Lesson Studio (`/lesson/[id]`):**
   - **Slide Player Mode:** Centered 2K viewport with click navigation, auto-slideshow, and bottom filmstrip.
   - **Scroll Storyboard Mode:** Continuous vertical narrative with inline note-taking per slide.
   - **Grid Overview Mode:** Bird's eye visual inspection.
   - **HD 2K Inspector:** Full viewport interactive pan/zoom (up to 400%) for examining micro-details.
   - **Notes & Bookmarks:** Slide-specific retention and quick revision.
